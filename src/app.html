      How it works, what is real, privacy

      A safety professional sees a dozen things on a floor walk. Today each one is typed into a different system hours later, and the near misses, at-risk behaviors and spoken promises are what get lost. Walkdown takes one capture, in any form, and turns it into the right linked records across the systems a site already uses.

      How a capture becomes a plan

      1. CaptureNotes typed or dictated, photos, short video and photos of documents such as permits.
      2. Read the mediaVideo is sampled into frames. The AI writes down what each photo, frame and document shows, including any written text.
      3. Understand and planThe AI splits the capture into events and proposes steps in each system: records, work orders, permit checks, training and alerts, with links between them.
      4. CheckRules in the app test every value before you see it. The list is below.
      5. ReviewYou answer follow-up questions, fix values and approve or skip each step. Immediate alerts can go first.
      6. SendSteps go out in order. Each system's new ID is written into the records that depend on it, failures queue and retry, and every action is logged.

      Checks on every plan

      • Every value must quote the capture. If the quote is not there, the value is removed and you see what was suggested.
      • Values must come from each system's pick-lists and the site's location list.
      • Names and numbers must appear inside their own quote.
      • Severity, recordability, regulator reporting and priority are left for a person.
      • Behavior observations and training assignments never carry a worker's name.
      • Health details belong in restricted fields; anywhere else they are flagged.
      • Judging wording such as “careless” is flagged for a neutral rewrite.
      • A keyword rule backs up the AI on serious-injury precursors.
      • A scan of the raw capture raises an immediate alert for words like ambulance or hospital, even if the AI missed it.
      • Sentences that did not reach any step are underlined, so nothing drops out silently.

      What is real and what is mocked

      Real

      The checks, the plan ordering, linking and retries, and the review flow run live. Inside Claude, the AI reads your own notes, photos and video frames.

      Mocked

      The five systems are simulated in your browser. Outside Claude, the AI part of each scenario is a recorded result, replayed. Video sound is not transcribed yet.

      Privacy

      In the public demo nothing leaves your browser. In live mode inside Claude, the capture goes to Claude on your own account. Records in the simulated systems stay in this browser. Please use made-up details.
